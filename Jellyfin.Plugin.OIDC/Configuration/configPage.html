OIDC RBAC Configuration

Access

Who may sign in at all — evaluated before role mapping. Leave the list empty to admit everyone who authenticates.

When enabled, existing local Jellyfin accounts that log in via OIDC will have their authentication provider switched to SSO automatically. Leave off if you want users to opt in manually.

When enabled, no provider's Authority may resolve to an RFC1918 private range (10/8, 172.16/12, 192.168/16) or IPv6 ULA (fc00::/7), for stricter network isolation. Off by default, since self-hosted IdPs commonly live on these ranges. Loopback and link-local addresses are always blocked regardless of this setting, unless individually allowed per-provider.
